MODEL PROVENANCE
The supplied model is a fine-tuned derivative of
sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2,
from Sentence Transformers / Hugging Face, based on Microsoft's MiniLM.
Upstream model card: https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2
Upstream license: Apache-2.0. The weights are distributed under Apache-2.0,
not under the packaging source code's MPL-2.0 license.
The model was modified by fine-tuning, vocabulary trimming and quantization.
Release metadata records the training source commit and export configuration.
See MODEL_LICENSE.txt for the Apache-2.0 license text.

The matching tokenizer is distributed with each model. Apple Core ML and
Foundation are system frameworks and are not redistributed.
